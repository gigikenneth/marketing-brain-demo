# Launch checklist

1. Write the brief in `campaigns/<name>/brief.md`.
2. Agree the message with product.
3. Draft the launch post with `prompts/blog-outline.md`.
4. Draft five LinkedIn posts with `prompts/linkedin-post.md`.
5. Book a webinar slot.
6. Email the newsletter list.
7. Write results in `campaigns/<name>/results.md` after six weeks.
