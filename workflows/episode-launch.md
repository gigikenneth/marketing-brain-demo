# Episode launch checklist

1. Edit the episode and fix the captions.
2. Write show notes with `prompts/podcast-show-notes.md`.
3. Publish to YouTube and Spotify.
4. Publish the blog post on asele.tech.
5. Share on LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok, tagging the guest.
