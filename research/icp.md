# Ideal customer profile

## Company

- Creative, digital or brand agency
- 15 to 150 people
- Mix of retainers and project work

## Buyer

Operations lead or head of delivery. Often the person who owns the resourcing spreadsheet.

## Triggers

- Two or more projects overran in the last quarter
- Hiring a second project manager
- Moving from one office to hybrid

## What they read

Agency network newsletters, LinkedIn posts from other ops leads, `research/customer-interviews-2026-q1.md` has quotes.
