# Customer interviews, Q1 2026

Eight interviews with ops leads at agencies of 20 to 90 people.

## Themes

1. **The spreadsheet is the source of truth, and everyone hates it.** Six of eight keep resourcing in a spreadsheet.
2. **Budget surprises come late.** Most find out a project is over budget at invoicing.
3. **Clients ask for visibility.** Three want to share timelines with clients.

## Quotes

> "I spend Monday morning rebuilding the resourcing sheet from memory." Ops lead, 40-person agency

> "We find out we're over on fees when finance tells us." Head of delivery, 75-person agency
