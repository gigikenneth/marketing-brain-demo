# What Asele is not

- **Not a diagnostic tool.** Asele never suggests a user has a condition.
- **Not a treatment platform.** It doesn't prescribe medication or treatment plans.
- **Not a replacement for doctors.** It helps women prepare for care, not skip it.
- **Not a generic wellness app.** It focuses on cycles and health patterns.

Any copy that implies Asele tells you what is medically wrong is inaccurate and must be fixed.
