# App store copy

## Subtitle

Track your cycle, spot patterns

## Description

Asele helps you track your cycle and symptoms, notice patterns over time and prepare for your next doctor's appointment.

- Daily check-ins in under 30 seconds
- Patterns across your cycles
- A Health Brief to share with your doctor
- Ask Amara in Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Swahili, French or English

Asele does not diagnose medical conditions.
