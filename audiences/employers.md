# Employers

HR and people leads at companies with women on the team.

## What they care about

- Supporting women's health without seeing anyone's personal data.
- A benefit people actually use.
- Simple setup.

## How we talk to them

Lead with privacy: the company sees aggregate usage only, never individual health data. Keep it practical and avoid wellness jargon.
