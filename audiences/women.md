# Primary audience: women managing their health

Women aged 22 to 35 in Nigerian cities or diaspora hubs. They use their phone for most things and have probably tried a period tracker before.

## What they want

- To know whether what they feel each month is normal or worth acting on.
- Evidence to bring to a doctor so they aren't dismissed.
- Guidance that fits what they eat and how they live.

## What they don't want

- To be treated as a fertility statistic.
- Wellness clichés.
