# Diaspora audience

Nigerian and Ghanaian women aged 28 to 42 living in the UK, Netherlands, Canada and the US. They navigate a health system that often doesn't reflect their experience, and value health guidance that does.

Speak to them about being heard in appointments and finding guidance that understands where they come from.
