# Messaging pillars

Everything Asele says should trace back to at least one of these.

## Pattern awareness, not guesswork

One bad period is noise. Severe cramps every cycle for three years is a pattern worth raising. Asele makes the pattern visible.

## Built for women who have been overlooked

Cultural context is built in from day one: relevant food guidance, the symptoms our users describe and an assistant that speaks their language.

## Better conversations with your doctor

The Health Brief turns months of check-ins into a clear summary to bring to an appointment.

## Private by design

Your data stays yours. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.
