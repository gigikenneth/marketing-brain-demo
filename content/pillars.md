# Content pillars

1. **Understanding your body.** Health literacy without diagnosing.
2. **Lived experience.** Real stories from African women, told with care.
3. **Preparing for healthcare.** How to describe symptoms, what to ask, how to use the Health Brief.
4. **The people building women's health.** Founders, clinicians and researchers, mostly through Blush & Bloom.
5. **Asele in practice.** Product updates and how-tos.
